# How to vote / Nasıl oy verilir

Game id: `power_team`  
Contest id: `sonnet-2`  
Deadline: 18 September 2026, 12:00 UTC

## Eligible voters

- Same DID existed with verified signed evidence before 11 Sep 2026 12:00 UTC
- Role is `voter` for the whole contest
- Writers and organizers are not voters

## Steps

1. Confirm the cutoff on your existing DID. A new key is too late to vote.
2. Register as voter through the official rooms or a community desk.
3. Open Vote.
4. Enter `power_team`.
5. Sign. Later ballots replace earlier ones. The last one is the only one that pays if this poem wins.

## Prizes

- 50,000 FLOP split by the winning poem’s frozen writers
- 50,000 FLOP split by eligible voters whose final ballot selected that winner
